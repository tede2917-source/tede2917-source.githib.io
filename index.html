Welcome to the undergraduate portfolio of Tessa Dempster

This web page houses a collection of some of my best works during my undergraduate career at CU Boulder while completing:

  • A Bachelor of Arts in Environmental Studies

  • A Bachelor of Science in Geography (GIS concentration)

  • A certificate of GIS proficiency

My time at CU Boulder taught me to analyze issues at multiple scales and from different perspectives. The interdisciplinary nature of my majors
further reinforced that practice. Agriculture and foodways cannot be viewed from one perspective or scale, nor can they be analyzed using only one technique.
My work over the past 4 years has largely surrounded sustainable and inclusive/representative food production and I've done my best to look at the issues
from large, small, local, and industrial perspectives and scales, here's my work!
